1. Introduction

LeakGrader Inc. ("LeakGrader", "we", "us", or "our") operates the website https://leakgrader.com and associated autonomous B2B intelligence services. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, process, store, and protect your information when you use our website, diagnostic tools, and AI Sales Closer bots.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Audit Requests: Website URLs or company names submitted for automated revenue leak diagnostic testing.
  • Account & Contact Info: Email address, name, phone number, and billing preferences when you unlock a full diagnostic report or subscribe to a paid tier.
  • Consultation & Lead Inquiries: Contact information entered into our 24/7 AI Sales Closer or booked calendar slots.

B. Publicly Available Commercial Intelligence

  • Public business registries, publicly accessible corporate websites, and professional contact databases used strictly for B2B commercial outreach synthesis.

C. Automatically Collected Technical Telemetry

  • IP address (anonymized for geolocation market matching), browser type, referring URL, operating system, and performance latency telemetry.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process collected information for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • To generate 15-point website diagnostic audits, speed benchmarks, and after-hours financial leak calculations.
  • To configure and run the autonomous 24/7 AI WhatsApp Closer bot and CRM qualification workflows.
  • To fulfill transactions, process payments via certified processors (LemonSqueezy, Stripe), and prevent fraudulent usage.
  • To comply with regulatory obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

4. Data Storage & Security Standards

We maintain rigorous administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3, and data at rest is secured via AES-256. We do not store raw credit card credentials on our servers; all payment transactions are handled through PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateways.

5. GDPR & CCPA Compliance (Your Rights)

Regardless of your geographic location, LeakGrader extends the following universal privacy rights to all users:

  • Right to Access: You may request a machine-readable copy of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request corrections to inaccurate personal records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You may request permanent deletion of your data from our active databases and archives.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You may opt-out of marketing communications at any time by clicking "unsubscribe" or notifying our data protection officer.
  • Do Not Sell My Info: We never sell, trade, or rent personal consumer information to third-party data brokers.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use strictly necessary cookies for session maintenance, currency preferences, and aggregated Google Analytics 4 (GA4) telemetry. You can control or disable cookie storage through your browser settings.
